About the South Coast

The southern coastline of Sri Lanka is a paradise of golden sands, swaying palm trees, and turquoise waters. From the historic charm of Galle Fort to the whale-watching adventures in Mirissa, this region offers a diverse range of experiences for every traveler.

Whether you're looking for world-class surfing, vibrant nightlife, or secluded coves where you can truly disconnect, the South Coast delivers. The area is also famous for its stilt fishermen, traditional mask carving, and some of the best seafood in the country.

Best Time to Visit

The best time to visit the South Coast is from **November to April**, when the weather is dry and the seas are calm. This period is ideal for swimming, snorkeling, and whale watching. During the rest of the year, while there might be more rain, the region remains beautiful and less crowded, perfect for those seeking a more tranquil escape.
