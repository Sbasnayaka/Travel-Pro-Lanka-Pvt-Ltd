Hill Country Scenic Tour

🕒 4 Days / 3 Nights 📍 Kandy, Ella, Nuwara Eliya ⭐ 5.0 (48 Reviews)

Overview

Experience the soul of Sri Lanka's central highlands. This journey takes you through emerald-green tea plantations, mist-covered mountains, and breathtaking waterfalls. You'll ride the world-famous "blue train," visit sacred temples, and enjoy the cool mountain air of Nuwara Eliya.

Designed for those who love nature, culture, and slow travel, this package offers a perfect blend of adventure and relaxation in some of the most beautiful landscapes on Earth.

Itinerary

Day 1: Arrival & The Sacred City of Kandy

Arrival at Colombo airport and transfer to Kandy. Visit the Temple of the Tooth Relic, enjoy a traditional cultural dance show, and take a peaceful evening walk around Kandy Lake.

Day 2: Tea Trails & Nuwara Eliya

Depart for Nuwara Eliya, known as "Little England." Stop at Ramboda Falls and visit a lush tea plantation. Experience the colonial charm and cool climate of the central hills.

Day 3: The Famous Train Ride to Ella

Board the morning train for a scenic journey to Ella. Pass through tunnels and over bridges with endless views of tea hills. Afternoon visit to the Nine Arches Bridge.

Day 4: Ella Peaks & Departure

A morning hike to Little Adam's Peak followed by a visit to Rawana Falls. Transfer back to the airport or your next destination with unforgettable memories.
